## Micro Journal Rev.8: Melodica

<img src="./images/001.png" />

Micro Journal Rev.8: Melodica, explores a **reflective monochrome LCD** that becomes clearer in brighter light. Like e-ink, it works with ambient light rather than against it, but with a much faster refresh rate. With no backlight, the display feels calm and comfortable during long writing sessions, while remaining fast enough to keep up with your thoughts.

For me, this build feels like a natural conclusion to the Micro Journal journey: simple, readable, responsive, and deeply focused on writing.
