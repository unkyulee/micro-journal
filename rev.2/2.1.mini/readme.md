## Micro Journal Rev.2.1.mini: Baby Shark

<img src="./images/001.png" />

Micro Journal Rev.2.1: Baby Shark blends the tactile joy of mechanical typing with the power and flexibility of a tiny computer. It's a portable writing deck built around a wide, clear display and a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W, paired with a satisfying mechanical keyboard that makes every keystroke feel like heaven. Whether you draft essays, capture sudden insights, or keep a slow, deliberate journal, the cyberDeck turns typing into a ritual again.
